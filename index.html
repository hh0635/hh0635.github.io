Spy x Family: The Beginning of Operation Strix

The story of Spy x Family begins with a mission, not a family. Loid Forger, a top spy working under the codename “Twilight,” is assigned Operation Strix, a critical mission meant to keep peace between two rival nations.

This leads him to adopt Anya, not out of affection, but because she fits the requirements for the mission. To Loid, she is originally just a necessary part of his plan—a way to get inside the academy and complete Operation Strix.

How Loid Met Yor

Loid meets Yor by coincidence while both are trying to maintain their own secret lives. Yor needs a fake partner to avoid suspicion at work, and Loid needs someone to act as Anya’s mother for Eden Academy interviews.
